I am a Ph.D. candidate in the **Construction Automation, Robotics, and Ergonomics (CARE) Lab** at Purdue University's Bowen School of Construction, advised by Dr. Yunfeng (Cindy) Chen. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

My dissertation develops a computer vision and multi-sensor framework for human pose estimation and integrated workplace risk assessment in field maintenance tasks. Working directly with highway maintenance crews at the Indiana Department of Transportation, I combine markerless motion capture, wearable physiological sensing, and field-based usability studies to measure how interventions such as passive back exoskeletons and ergonomic handles change the physical demands of real work.

Before Purdue, I earned my M.S. in Civil Engineering (Project Management) and B.S. in Civil Engineering from Wuhan University, where my research used network analysis to study how organizations coordinate under delay and disruption in complex projects.

## Research Interests

Construction Safety · Human Factors · Human-Computer Interaction · Computer Vision · Wearable Sensing · Artificial Intelligence · Workforce Development · Work Zone Safety · Building Information Modeling
